## Hobby community purpose

The Service exists so drivers can record telemetry, fork public sheets onto chassis they own, and discuss hobby-grade RC crawlers and racers. It is not a marketplace, not a sanctioned racing series, and not professional engineering advice.

## User-generated setup sheets

Setup titles, notes, product URLs, and telemetry values are user-generated content. Drivers are responsible for what they publish. Public sheets may be inspected, forked, liked, and reported by other authenticated drivers.

## No warranty of mechanical safety

Setup numbers (final drive ratio, shock oil, corner weights, and similar) are community notes, not a certified bill of materials. The operators provide **no warranty** that any logged configuration is mechanically safe, legal for a particular event, or suitable for any vehicle. You remain responsible for inspecting hardware, following manufacturer instructions, and not operating a model in a way that can injure people or property.

## Moderation

Operators may hide public setup sheets and suspend accounts that violate these Terms or the Community Guidelines. Hide and suspend actions are recorded in an internal audit log. Hidden sheets drop out of the community feed and public QR inspection.

## Account deletion

You may delete your account from driver settings after confirming with your password. Deletion removes your profile, vehicles, and setup sheets. Forked copies that other drivers already own may remain, with parent lineage pointers cleared. There is currently no outbound mailer, so account recovery by email is not offered.
