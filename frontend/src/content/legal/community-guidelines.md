These guidelines apply to everyone using the RC Car & Rock Crawler Garage & Setup Logger. They sit beside the Terms of Service. Contact [operators@localhost](mailto:operators@localhost).

## What this community is for

Share hobby RC setup sheets: gearing, shock oil, tire compounds, corner weights, and trail notes. Inspect public sheets, fork them onto a chassis you own, and keep your own garage honest.

## Setup-sheet integrity

Do not spam the feed with junk sheets, malware or phishing product URLs, or copies of someone else's unique tune presented as your original work when you are asked not to. Stolen-setup reports exist so operators can hide sheets that cross that line.

## Conduct

Do not abuse other drivers in titles, notes, bios, or callsigns. Harassment, hate, and threats are not part of pit-mat culture here. Operators may hide content and suspend accounts.

## Reports, not a social graph

Use **Report sheet** and **Report driver** for spam, abuse, stolen setups, or malware links. This is not a mute list, block list, appeal portal, or direct-message product. Moderators review an open queue on the Scrutineering Desk.

## Age

You must be 13 or older. Registration requires that attestation.

## Safety reminder

Logged numbers are not a warranty. Confirm fasteners, battery handling, and event rules yourself before you run.

## Enforcement

Hidden sheets leave the public feed and QR inspection. Suspended accounts cannot use authenticated routes until reinstated. Repeat or severe abuse can lead to account deletion by the driver or removal by operators.
